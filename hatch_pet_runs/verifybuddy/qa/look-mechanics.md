# VerifyBuddy look mechanics

VerifyBuddy is a humanoid chibi sticker pet, so gaze direction should come from eyes, eyelids, eyebrows, head/neck turn, and a restrained upper-body follow-through. Do not stretch or warp the skull, shirt, hands, charm, or shoes. The feet/lower body stay visually anchored, while the head and upper torso lead subtly.

Cardinal pose families:

- `000 up`: pupils and eyelids lift upward, brows rise, chin tips slightly up, upper torso stays nearly front-facing.
- `090 screen-right`: pupils, nose, mouth, and head turn toward the viewer's right; viewer-right cheek and ear become more readable, torso follows slightly.
- `180 down`: eyes and head angle downward, brows soften, chin lowers toward the shirt/charm, body remains centered.
- `270 screen-left`: pupils, nose, mouth, and head turn toward the viewer's left; viewer-left cheek and ear become more readable, torso follows slightly.

Intermediate directions interpolate smoothly between those cardinal pose families in 22.5-degree steps. Preserve the black-and-gold outfit, neural-verification charm, rounded body proportions, sticker outline, and mischievous expression family. The charm is worn on the shirt and follows the torso; it should not detach, float, or flip randomly.
